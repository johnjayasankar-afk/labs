03What they share

No demos. The rule each build keeps.

  • 01 · RideLens

    Ranges stay ranges.

    When a fare is a range, both ends stay on the board. RideLens never invents a midpoint to look decisive.

  • 02 · Daylight

    Success is not evidence.

    Daylight separates what it asked for, what macOS accepted, and what it could read back and confirm.

  • 03 · RailDrop

    Never an invented price.

    If the live board is down, RailDrop says so. It never guesses a fare, a change fee, or an itinerary.

  • 04 · Gridiron

    Reported, never guessed.

    Every spot, clock, and chance comes from a source that reported it. A missing ball spot says so; it is never guessed to midfield.

  • 05 · AgentFit

    Fit is not autonomy.

    A workflow can score in the eighties and still warrant nothing beyond human-approved execution.

  • 06 · Cartonry

    Not a picture.

    The 3D fold is the geometry the dieline is cut from, and the tests fold the net and measure it against the dimensions typed.

  • 07 · KeepFloor

    Every published fee, counted.

    The whole published fee stack, including Offsite Ads, then the lowest charmed list price that survives it.

  • 08 · Pricing Hub

    Plain formulas only.

    No subscription, no add-ins, no macros. Every link between sheets is a real formula reference.
